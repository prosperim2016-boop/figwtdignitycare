ABOUT US

Helping You Find The Right Carer

At FiGwt Dignity Care, our aim is to make finding suitable care support simpler for individuals and families.

We introduce clients to suitable care workers based on their requirements, preferences and circumstances.

We believe that finding the right person to support you should be based on dignity, respect, choice and compatibility.

OUR SERVICES

What We Do

We provide an introduction and matching service between individuals, families and suitable care workers.

👤

Carer Matching

We help identify care workers whose experience and availability may match the client's requirements.

🤝

Client Introductions

We facilitate introductions between clients, families and suitable care workers.

📋

Information & Guidance

We provide information to help clients understand the introduction process and make informed choices.

🏠

Home-Based Support Matching

We help individuals looking for suitable independent care workers to support them in their own homes.

❤️

Personalised Matching

We consider individual preferences, location, experience and availability when making introductions.

🔎

Carer Network

We maintain a network of care workers interested in being introduced to individuals seeking support.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple. Personal. Dignified.

1

Tell Us What You Need

Contact us and tell us about the type of care worker you are looking for.

2

We Search Our Network

We identify potential care workers who may match your requirements.

3

Introduction

We introduce you to suitable care workers so that you can discuss your requirements directly.

4

You Make the Choice

You decide whether the care worker is suitable for your needs.

FOR CARE WORKERS

Looking for Care Opportunities?

If you are an experienced care worker looking for opportunities to be introduced to individuals and families seeking care support, you can register your interest with FiGwt Dignity Care.

Register Your Interest

Important Information

FiGwt Dignity Care operates as an introductory service. We introduce individuals and families to care workers and do not provide or manage personal care services through this introductory service.

Clients and care workers should establish their own arrangements and responsibilities following an introduction.
